FAIRLY is a front-end demonstration of a fair discovery and ticket booking experience — designed to make exploring events feel friendly, clear, and fun.

🎪

Our idea

Great fairs bring people together. FAIRLY gives visitors a simple place to browse experiences, compare entry prices, and keep their booking details organised.

This prototype uses sample event data and browser storage. It does not process payments or issue real admission tickets.

🧭

How it works

  1. Browse fairs and use category filters.
  2. Open an event and choose your ticket quantity.
  3. Enter attendee details and review the total.
  4. Find your saved booking under My tickets.
Good to know

Built for clarity

🔒

Privacy-conscious demo

Booking information is saved locally in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive or real payment details.

📱

Responsive by design

Layouts adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops, with a mobile navigation bar on smaller screens.

🛠

Ready to extend

A production version would need a backend, database, authentication, payment provider, and ticket validation.
